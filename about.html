About Calqin.com

Calqin.com is a free online calculator platform built to help Indians make quick, accurate financial and everyday calculations — without any signup, ad clutter, or confusion. We believe that useful tools should be accessible to everyone, everywhere, on any device.

Our Mission

To provide the fastest, most accurate, and most accessible calculators for GST, loans, investments, health, and daily needs — completely free, forever. We want to be the first place you think of when you need a quick calculation.

Why We Built This

Most calculator websites are cluttered with intrusive ads, slow to load, and frustrating on mobile. Many require signups or push notifications just to use a basic tool. We wanted to build a clean, fast, mobile-first alternative that just works — no distractions, no tricks.

What We Offer

We currently offer 30+ calculators across four categories:

  • Finance: GST, EMI, SIP, FD, PPF, HRA, Income Tax, Salary, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, NPS, Lumpsum, CAGR, Gratuity, Loan Eligibility, Car Loan EMI, Personal Loan EMI, Discount, Tip
  • Health: BMI, BMI for Kids, Calorie, Water Intake, Pregnancy Due Date
  • Everyday: Age, Percentage, Date Difference
  • Converters: Unit, Currency, Temperature

Our Principles

  • Free Forever: All calculators are 100% free. No premium tiers, no hidden charges.
  • Privacy First: All calculations run in your browser. We don't store your data.
  • No Signup: Use any calculator instantly without creating an account.
  • Mobile-First: Every tool works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Accuracy: Our formulas follow official standards and are regularly reviewed.

Who Uses Calqin.com?

Our users include students, business owners, freelancers, salaried employees, home buyers, investors, fitness enthusiasts, and anyone who needs a quick calculation. Whether you're calculating GST on an invoice, planning a home loan EMI, or checking your BMI — we've got you covered.

Our Team

Calqin.com is built and maintained by a small team of developers and finance enthusiasts based in India. We're passionate about building simple, useful tools that make everyday life easier.
