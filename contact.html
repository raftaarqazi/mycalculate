Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is calqin.com free to use?
Yes, all our calculators are 100% free. No signup, no payment, no hidden charges.

Q: Do you store the numbers I enter?
No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. We do not store, collect, or transmit any data you enter.

Q: Can I suggest a new calculator?
Absolutely! Email us with your suggestion and we'll consider adding it.

Q: Do you offer API access?
Not currently. But we may offer it in the future. Contact us if you're interested.
