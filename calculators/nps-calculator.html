What is NPS?

NPS (National Pension Scheme) is a government-sponsored retirement savings scheme. It allows you to invest regularly during your working years and receive a pension after retirement. NPS is regulated by PFRDA.

NPS Key Features

  • Entry Age: 18 to 70 years
  • Minimum Contribution: ₹500/month or ₹1,000/year
  • Lock-in: Until age 60
  • Tax Benefit: Additional ₹50,000 deduction under 80CCD(1B)
  • Returns: Market-linked (depends on fund choice)

NPS Investment Options

  • Equity (E): Up to 75%, highest returns
  • Corporate Bonds (C): Medium risk
  • Government Securities (G): Low risk
  • Alternative Assets (A): Real estate, REITs

At Retirement (Age 60)

  • Minimum 40% must be used to buy annuity (monthly pension)
  • Up to 60% can be withdrawn tax-free as lump sum
  • Annuity income is taxable as per slab

Example: ₹5,000/month for 25 years at 10%

  • Total invested: ₹15,00,000
  • Estimated corpus: ~₹66,00,000
  • Lump sum (60%): ₹39,60,000
  • Annuity (40%): ₹26,40,000
