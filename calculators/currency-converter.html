Currency Conversion Basics

Currency conversion helps you convert one currency into another using the current exchange rate. Rates fluctuate constantly based on global economic conditions, central bank policies, and market sentiment.

Common Currency Pairs

  • USD/INR: Most commonly tracked by Indians
  • EUR/INR: Euro to Rupee
  • GBP/INR: Pound to Rupee
  • USD/EUR: Global reserve pair

Where Currency Conversion is Used

  • International travel — plan your budget
  • Online shopping from foreign sites
  • Freelancers receiving foreign payments
  • NRIs sending money to India
  • Students studying abroad
  • Import/export business

Note on Exchange Rates

Our calculator uses approximate rates for quick reference. For actual transactions, banks and forex dealers add a markup of 1-3% above the interbank rate. Always check live rates before making transactions.

Factors Affecting Exchange Rates

  • Interest rate differentials between countries
  • Inflation rates
  • Current account balance
  • Political stability
  • Central bank policies
  • Global economic events
