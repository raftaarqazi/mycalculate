What is a Date Difference Calculator?

A date difference calculator tells you the exact number of days, weeks, and months between two dates. It's useful for planning events, tracking projects, calculating age, or counting down to special occasions.

Where It's Useful

  • Event planning: Days until wedding, birthday, exam
  • Project management: Timeline tracking
  • Pregnancy: Track weeks of pregnancy
  • Legal: Contract durations, notice periods
  • Financial: Loan tenure, investment periods
  • Personal: Days until vacation, retirement

How Date Difference is Calculated

The difference is calculated by converting both dates to milliseconds (Unix time) and taking the absolute difference:

Days = |Date2 − Date1| ÷ (1000 × 60 × 60 × 24)

Weeks = Days ÷ 7. Months = Days ÷ 30.44 (average month length).

Example

From 1 January 2026 to 15 March 2026:

  • Days: 73
  • Weeks: 10.4
  • Months: 2.4

Leap Years Considered

Our calculator automatically accounts for leap years (2024, 2028, 2032, etc.), ensuring accurate day counts even over long periods.
