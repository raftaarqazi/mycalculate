What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps you figure out the final price of a product after applying a percentage discount, and how much money you're saving. Essential during sales, festive offers, or any time you're shopping.

How Discount is Calculated

Savings = Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Savings

Example: A shirt costs ₹1,000 with 20% off → Savings = ₹200, Final Price = ₹800.

Stacked Discounts (Discount on Discount)

Sometimes stores offer "extra 10% off on already discounted items". This is NOT 30% off — it's calculated on the already-reduced price.

Example: ₹1,000 shirt, 20% off → ₹800, then extra 10% off → ₹720. Total discount = 28%, not 30%.

  • Always compare final price, not discount %
  • Watch for "up to X% off" — actual discount may be lower
  • Check if same product is cheaper elsewhere
  • Festive sales often have best deals on specific categories
