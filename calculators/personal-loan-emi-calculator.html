What is a Personal Loan?

A personal loan is an unsecured loan — no collateral required. Banks offer it based on your income and credit score. Interest rates are higher than secured loans because there's no asset backing.

Personal Loan Highlights

  • Loan Amount: ₹50,000 to ₹50 lakh
  • Tenure: 1 to 5 years (some up to 7)
  • Interest Rate: 10.5% to 24%
  • Processing Fee: 1-3% of loan
  • Disbursement: Usually within 24-72 hours

Example: ₹2,00,000 at 14% for 2 years

  • Monthly EMI: ~₹9,600
  • Total interest: ~₹30,400
  • Total payment: ~₹2,30,400

When to Take a Personal Loan

  • Medical emergency
  • Wedding expenses
  • Home renovation
  • Travel
  • Debt consolidation
  • Education (if education loan is not an option)

When to Avoid Personal Loan

  • For luxury purchases or gadgets
  • For investing in stocks or mutual funds
  • If you can't afford the EMI
  • For gambling or speculative activities

Tips to Get Best Personal Loan

  • Maintain credit score of 750+
  • Compare offers across banks and NBFCs
  • Negotiate on interest rate and processing fee
  • Choose shorter tenure if EMI is affordable
  • Prepay when you have surplus cash
