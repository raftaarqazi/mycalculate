What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator tells you your exact age in years, months, and days from your date of birth to today (or any other date). While simple age is just years, an accurate age calculation considers leap years, month lengths, and exact days.

How Age is Calculated

Age calculation involves subtracting your date of birth from the current date. The tricky part is handling different month lengths and leap years:

  • If today's day is less than your birth day, borrow days from the previous month (28, 29, 30, or 31 depending on the month)
  • If today's month is less than your birth month, borrow one year and add 12 months
  • Total days are calculated by dividing the difference in milliseconds by 86,400,000

Common Uses of Age Calculator

  • Government forms — Exact age in years, months, days required for many applications
  • School admission — Age criteria often needs exact calculation
  • Retirement planning — Know exactly when you'll hit retirement age
  • Job applications — Age proof for eligibility
  • Medical purposes — Pediatric dosage often depends on exact age
  • Just curiosity — Know how many days you've lived!
