What is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you quickly figure out how much tip to leave at a restaurant, café, or for any service, and how to split the total bill among friends. No more mental math at the table!

How Much Tip Should You Leave?

Standard tipping norms in India:

  • 5-10% — Average service
  • 10-15% — Good service
  • 15-20% — Excellent service
  • ₹20-50 — Small bills (chai, snacks, delivery)

In India, tipping is optional but appreciated. Unlike the US, servers here aren't dependent on tips for their income.

Why Tipping Matters

Tipping rewards good service, encourages better hospitality, and shows appreciation for hardworking staff. In many restaurants, tips are pooled and shared among the entire staff — servers, kitchen, and support crew.

Splitting the Bill

Our calculator makes it easy to split bills between any number of people. Just enter the total, tip %, and number of people — get per-person amount instantly. Perfect for group dinners, office lunches, and trips.

Tip Around the World

  • USA: 15-20% expected — servers depend on tips
  • UK: 10-15% (often included as service charge)
  • Europe: 5-10% or rounding up
  • Japan: Not customary — may be considered rude
  • India: Optional, 5-10% for good service
  • Middle East: 10-15% expected

Tips for Tipping in India

  • Check if "service charge" is already included in the bill
  • For delivery apps, ₹20-50 is standard depending on order size
  • For salon, spa, or hotel staff, ₹50-200 depending on service
  • Cash tips are preferred by most service staff
  • For large groups, 10% is a good default
