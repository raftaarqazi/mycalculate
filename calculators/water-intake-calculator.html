Why Water Intake Matters

Water makes up 60% of your body. It's essential for digestion, circulation, temperature regulation, joint lubrication, and removing waste. Even mild dehydration (1-2%) affects energy, mood, and cognitive performance.

How Much Water Do You Need?

The general recommendation is 8 glasses (2 liters) per day. But this varies based on:

  • Body weight
  • Physical activity
  • Climate (hot weather = more water)
  • Health conditions (pregnancy, illness)
  • Diet (caffeine, salt, protein intake)

Precise Formula

Daily Water (liters) = Weight (kg) × 0.033

Plus extra for exercise: 350-500 ml per 30 minutes of activity.

Example

70 kg person, 30 min exercise:

  • Base = 70 × 0.033 = 2.31 L
  • Exercise = 0.35 L
  • Total = 2.66 L/day (~10-11 glasses)

Signs of Dehydration

  • Dark yellow urine
  • Dry mouth and lips
  • Fatigue and dizziness
  • Headache
  • Poor concentration
  • Constipation

Tips to Stay Hydrated

  • Carry a water bottle everywhere
  • Drink a glass after every meal
  • Set reminders on your phone
  • Add lemon or mint for taste
  • Eat water-rich foods (watermelon, cucumber, oranges)
