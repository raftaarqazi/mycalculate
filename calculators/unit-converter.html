Why Use a Unit Converter?

Different countries use different measurement systems. India uses the metric system (km, kg, °C) while the US uses imperial (miles, pounds, °F). A unit converter makes it easy to switch between these systems quickly and accurately, without memorizing complex formulas.

Common Unit Conversions

Length:

  • 1 km = 0.621371 miles
  • 1 mile = 1.60934 km
  • 1 meter = 3.28084 feet
  • 1 foot = 0.3048 meters
  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm

Weight:

  • 1 kg = 2.20462 pounds
  • 1 pound = 0.453592 kg
  • 1 ounce = 28.3495 grams

Temperature:

  • °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • K = °C + 273.15

When You Need Unit Conversion

  • Traveling internationally (distance, temperature)
  • Cooking with foreign recipes (weight, volume)
  • Fitness tracking (kg vs lb)
  • Engineering and scientific work
  • Online shopping from foreign websites
  • Education and homework
  • Reading international news and weather reports

Metric vs Imperial Systems

Metric (SI): Based on multiples of 10 — used by almost all countries. Easy conversions. Units: km, m, cm, kg, g, °C, liter.

Imperial: Used mainly in USA, UK, and a few others. Units: mile, yard, foot, inch, pound, °F, gallon.

Most of the world uses metric. The US is the only major country that primarily uses imperial for everyday measurements.

Quick Memory Tricks

  • 1 km ≈ 0.6 miles (rough estimate)
  • 1 kg ≈ 2.2 pounds
  • 1 meter ≈ 3.3 feet
  • Normal body temp: 37°C = 98.6°F
  • Water boils at 100°C = 212°F
